Questions
What file formats do I get? +
Budget calculators, timelines, and trackers come as Excel (.xlsx) files with working formulas. Proposals and loan agreements come as PDF. Some packs include both.
Can I edit the downloaded files? +
Yes. Every file is fully editable. Excel files have unlocked formulas. PDFs can be opened in any editor. These are your documents — customize them however you need.
Is this a subscription? +
No. You pay once and download your files. No recurring charges. The free timeline generator is always free, no credit card required.
Who built this? +
Vitrine was built by exhibition professionals who got tired of reinventing the same planning documents for every new project. The templates are based on real workflows from international exhibitions.
Will there be more languages? +
German and Turkish editions are in development. Sign up for the free tier and we'll notify you when they launch.
